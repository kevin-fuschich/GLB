# CLUB_PAGE_SPEC.md
## Global League Baseball — Club Placard System

### Purpose
Club Pages exist to explain **why a team exists**, not how it is performing.

They are **narrative documents**, designed to feel like museum placards, archival records, or institutional summaries.  
They intentionally contrast with Team Record pages, which function as statistical ledgers.

A reader should finish a Club Page understanding the **city, history, and cultural logic** that produced the team.

---

## Core Philosophy

- **Records are spreadsheets. Clubs are artifacts.**
- No page should feel promotional, celebratory, or nostalgic.
- The tone is documentary, restrained, and confident.
- Every element must justify its presence as *evidence*, not decoration.

---

## Structural Rules (Required)

### Page Type
- Single-column placard
- Max width: `580px`
- Centered on neutral field background
- Top border in GLB ink navy (`#002244`)

### Typography
- Body text: Serif-forward (`Georgia`, `Times New Roman`)
- Section labels: Sans-serif, uppercase, letter-spaced
- Smart quotes (“ ”) required throughout
- No decorative fonts

### Sections (In Order)
1. **Header**
2. **Identity**
3. **Grounds**
4. **Tradition**
5. **Timeline**
6. **Record Reference (Footer)**

No additional sections without system approval.

---

## Header Rules

### Title
- City name only (e.g., *San Pedro*, *Birmingham*)
- No mascots, nicknames, or slogans
