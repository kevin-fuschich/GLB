# GLB display advertisements

Advertisements lead with the advertiser's products and voice. Do not write club-tour or stadium-sponsorship explanations into the campaign copy. The About Our Sponsors page carries the brand definitions and entity claims.

The Stats carousel links to sponsor profiles, labels each item Advertisement, and retains pause, previous/next and reduced-motion behavior. Proof sheets are identity references, not finished advertising creative.

Three new product photographs were generated with the built-in image tool, then converted to WebP without compositional editing. VELLO retains its existing audio-ring product photograph. No generated image contains GLB/club names or sponsorship language.

## Image prompts

### magixo

Create one new photoreal product photograph for a MAGIXO detergent advertisement. Reference supplies exact approved fictional identity: bold italic cobalt Magixo wordmark, coral sparkle and underline, cobalt jug with coral cap and warm ivory label with coral panel. A single believable detergent jug on a cream laundry-room counter beside loosely folded white cotton towels, softly blurred washer behind. Warm natural window light, restrained tactile commercial photography, real plastic and fabric, generous breathing room, main jug and label centered and fully visible. Wide 3:2 landscape. NO collage, no giant separate logo header, no storefront panel, no stadium, no baseball, no GLB, no club name or sponsorship text. Only authentic product-label lettering 'Magixo' and 'DETERGENTE'; no added slogan or overlay text.

### mumbo-jumbo

Create one new photoreal product photograph for the fictional MUMBO JUMBO stationery brand. Reference supplies exact stacked heavy black MUMBO JUMBO wordmark with small red dot and the matte black pen with red clicker. Photograph an ivory pocket notebook, a second open dotted journal and the black Nano Ink pen on a warm pale stone writing desk in soft afternoon window light. Notebook cover contains ONLY MUMBO JUMBO wordmark and small red dot. Open journal has subtle plain dot grid, NO baseball scoring grid. Authentic paper texture, understated premium commercial photography, main products centered and fully visible with generous breathing room. Wide 3:2 landscape. NO Osaka wording, no team logo, no baseball, no stadium, no GLB, no scorebook, no separate graphic masthead, no collage, no overlay slogan.

### tumbao

Create one new photoreal product photograph for fictional outdoor clothing/equipment brand TUMBAO. Reference supplies exact established rolltop backpack design: clay fabric, glacier-blue panel, aubergine pockets, acid-yellow webbing, chunky black buckles, reinforced stitching, small ivory woven patch with black angular open-square symbol and TUMBAO wordmark with fully CLOSED O. The single backpack rests naturally against a weathered rock at the edge of an Andean trail; soft cloudy mountain landscape out of focus behind it. Grounded tactile commercial photography, no HDR, no theatrical sky. Backpack fully visible centered with generous breathing room, wide 3:2 landscape. NO collage or giant separate logo header, no stadium, no baseball, no GLB, no club connection or sponsor tie-in, no overlay slogan, no other brands.
