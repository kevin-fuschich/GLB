# VELLO First Circuit: crowd mix

Original tones and synthesized percussion: GLB.

Crowd and applause recordings by stephan, originally published by PDSounds and released into the public domain worldwide by the author, as documented on these Wikimedia Commons file pages:

- Festival Concert People Crowd: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Festival_concert_people_crowd.ogg
- Slow Starting Applause: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Slow_starting_applause.ogg

Edits: excerpts, overlapping ambience, band-pass filtering, light stereo diffusion, volume envelopes, fades, and mixing beneath the original GLB cue. These are audience textures in a fictional sound study, not a recording at VELLO Stadium.

The original clean mix remains at assets/audio/vello-first-circuit.mp3. The crowd mix is assets/audio/vello-first-circuit-crowd.mp3.
