Spokane Alloys Ballpark File

OGWA Stadium

Set along Spokane’s riverfront, OGWA Stadium combines modern Northwest architecture, sea-glass detailing, and a restrained industrial profile built around the Spokane Alloys.

Venue Overview

Opened in 2019, OGWA Stadium anchors the Spokane Alloys’ riverfront ballpark district with a design language built around concrete, steel, glass, water, and restrained sea-glass color. The park is modern without feeling oversized, premium without feeling detached, and built to feel like a functioning part of Spokane rather than a stadium dropped into it.

Known by fans as Hydro Field, the stadium is defined by its sea-glass concourse glow, river-facing approaches, and the Sea Glass Rain Wall bottle filling station — a practical fan amenity that has become one of the park’s most recognizable features.

Sea Glass Rain Wall

The Sea Glass Rain Wall is OGWA Stadium’s signature fan amenity: a concourse bottle filling station built into a textured glass water wall.

Fans use it to refill bottles during games, including the Spokane Alloys OGWA bottles distributed through stadium promotions. The feature is practical first, but its sound, texture, and filtered light have made it one of the park’s most photographed interior landmarks.

It is not treated as a spectacle. It is part of the building: useful, calm, durable, and specific to Spokane.
