The Club

The Aeros play under high desert light — a crowd that looks up as often as it looks in. Hot air balloons drift beyond the outfield, and the park feels like a civic event more than a venue: families, radios, late sun, and the steady noise of people who came to be together.

This page is filed as a club dossier artifact. It does not explain itself. It records itself.

Ballpark

Propellant Park. A bowl of light and sky — warm seats, open air, and a scoreboard that always feels a little too loud. When the balloons rise, the crowd points like it’s part of the game.

Broadcast & Announcer

Rafa “Skyline” Ortega

Play-by-play

Fast, warm, and local. Rafa calls games like he’s narrating the city — innings stitched together with weather, crowd rhythm, and the occasional balloon count.

Marla Keene

In-stadium announcer

The voice inside the park. Clean reads, zero drama, and a cadence that feels like tradition — the kind of presence you don’t notice until it’s missing.

Mascot & Stands

Mascot. Not a symbol — a regular. Slightly scuffed costume, big gestures, always in the aisle. High-fives are mandatory.

The Stands. Teal shirts, balloon patches, sunburned shoulders, kids on rails. It’s loud in a friendly way — like a neighborhood block party that happens to have baseball.
