The Club

The Vulcans play like a furnace runs: steady heat, no wasted motion, and a late-game surge that feels inevitable. Birmingham isn’t “industrial dystopia” — it’s craft and sweat and civic pride. The club treats every inning like a shift you finish clean.

  • Style: Pressure / Late Surges
  • Vibe: Foundry Heat
  • Tradition: “The Pour” (7th-inning rally)

Ballpark

Furnace Field. A brick-and-iron park with glowing light towers and an outfield backdrop of old stacks repurposed as landmarks. When the sun drops, the whole place turns copper.

Broadcast

Walt “Sparks” Dempsey

Voice of the Vulcans. Big-lung, blue-collar play-by-play with a soft spot for pitching craft. He calls strikeouts like they’re welds: clean, bright, and final.

Mascot & Stands

Slag

A friendly, soot-dusted creature in a riveted vest. Slag doesn’t menace — he works the aisles like a foreman, approving signs, handing out “heat checks,” and making the kids feel official.

The Spark Section

A standing rail of regulars who only get loud when it matters. Their rally clap starts slow, then spreads like ignition — one beat at a time.
