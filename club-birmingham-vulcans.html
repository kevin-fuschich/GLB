The Club

Birmingham is loud, physical, and unembarrassed about it. The Vulcans are a civic fixture — less spectacle than presence. Games feel like events the city expects to endure together.

Ballpark

Slag Field. Brick, steel, and heat. The park absorbs noise and throws it back. Night games glow under heavy lights and heavier air.

Broadcast & Announcers

Radio Broadcast

The voice on the wire. Calm, steady, unhurried. Calls the game like it’s meant to last.

In-Game Announcer

Lives inside the park. Names echo. Counts linger. The crowd listens whether they want to or not.

Mascot & Stands

Vulcan

A working mascot. Loud, physical, familiar. Always where the crowd is thickest.

The Stands

Families, shift workers, regulars. The noise builds slowly — then stays.
