On-Field

Where the baseball actually happens. Dirt on the pants. Pine tar on the hands. The kind of heat that makes shadows look heavier.

Mascot

A real, human-worn suit with history: scuffed seams, sweat marks, and the confidence of a character who’s been here forever.

The Stands

Families on berms, kids with sticky hands, coolers under seats — a crowd that looks like it drove in from everywhere around the valley.

Broadcast & Announcer two channels / one park

Sunny Jim

Radio Play-by-Play

Warm, unhurried calls that drift into weather and harvest talk. It plays like a neighbor narrating the evening from a porch that happens to overlook a diamond.

Marla “Bell” Rios

In-Stadium Announcer

Clean cadence, county-fair energy. Between innings she reads sponsor tags like folklore — and somehow the whole place listens.
