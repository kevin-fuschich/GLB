The Club

The Yield plays in a perpetual summer of abundance. Fresno overwhelms opponents through volume and pace, turning every inning into a harvest cycle. Games are loud, loose, and relentless — a team that never looks tired and never slows down.

  • Style: High Contact / Pressure
  • Vibe: Summer Abundance
  • Tradition: “The Bumper Crop”

Ballpark

The Corn Cathedral. Built directly into working farmland, the outfield is bordered by living crops. A home run is said to “feed the county.” The air smells of irrigation and cut grass.

Broadcast

Sunny Jim

Voice of the Yield. Broadcasting from an open-air gazebo, Jim’s call drifts like a farm report — warm, conversational, and never in a hurry.

Mascot & Stands

The Husk

A living construct of vines and stalks, The Husk embodies unchecked growth and summer vitality.

The Picnic Hills

Blankets, coolers, and families line the berms. Fresno games feel more like a county fair than a contest.
