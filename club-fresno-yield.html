The Club

The Yield plays baseball the way the valley grows crops: fast, dense, and without apology. Fresno overwhelms opponents with constant pressure and relentless contact, forcing mistakes through volume. Games here feel long, loud, and sunburned.

  • Style: High Contact / High Output
  • Vibe: Summer Abundance
  • Tradition: “The Bumper Crop”

Ballpark

The Corn Cathedral. The outfield dissolves directly into farmland. There is no true wall — just tall corn, orchards, and irrigation rigs. Home runs disappear into growth.

Broadcast

Sunny Jim

Voice of the Yield. Jim sounds like late afternoon. His calls drift between play-by-play, weather updates, and harvest talk. It feels less like a broadcast and more like someone talking to you over a fence.

Mascot & Stands

The Husk

A walking bundle of stalks and vines. The Husk represents growth without restraint. Leaves fall off him all game long.

The Picnic Hills

No seats. Blankets, coolers, kids running everywhere. It feels more like a county fair than a stadium — until the ball starts flying.
