The Cementeros

Monterrey is built on heavy industry, limestone, and heat. The Cementeros play baseball the way the city works—compressed, relentless, and without ornament. The game is not played. It is worked.

  • Style: Attrition
  • Vibe: Industrial Noise
  • Tradition: “5 PM Whistle”

Ballpark

Built into an abandoned steel mill footprint, Estadio Fundidora traps heat and momentum. Ground balls travel fast. Fly balls die early.

Broadcast

Héctor “El Arquitecto” Vega

Clinical, rhythmic, structural. “The foundation holds.”

Mascot & Stands

Cimiento. He does not move.

La Obra. Wrenches on steel. No cheering.
