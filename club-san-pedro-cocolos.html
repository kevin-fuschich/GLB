The Club

The San Pedro Cocolos play with heat and rhythm—fast hands, loud bats, and a confidence that spreads inning to inning. They don’t wait for momentum. They manufacture it: pressure on the bases, sharp contact, and a crowd that turns every big pitch into a drumbeat.

Ballpark

El Malecón. A waterfront park where the salt air hangs over the infield and the lights glow against the palm line. The stands sit close. Music leaks into the concourse. Late innings feel like a street festival that just happens to have baseball at its center.

Broadcast & Announcers

Luis “El Narrador” Peña

Play-by-play. Fast, musical, and crystal clear. Peña calls rallies like they’re climbing stairs— every pitch a step, every swing a landing. He lets the crowd sing through his pauses.

Maribel Santos

In-stadium announcer. Bright and commanding. Her voice cuts cleanly through the bowl—lineups, counts, substitutions, and promotions delivered with timing that keeps the park moving.

Mascot & Stands

Cocolo

A local legend in costume—more swagger than routine. Cocolo works the rail with high-fives, shoulder shimmies, and a slow, theatrical wave when the Cocolos take the lead.

The Wharf Side

Drums, flags, and chants that bounce off concrete. Fans arrive early, stay late, and treat every close game like a neighborhood event. When the ninth inning hits, the whole section leans forward together.
