San Pedro

Est. 2024 • Dominican Republic

Identity

The name “Cocolos” is a reclamation of history. It honors the Afro-Caribbean migrants from the English-speaking islands who arrived in San Pedro in the late 19th century to work the sugar mills.

They brought the English language, a distinct culture, and the game of cricket—which eventually gave way to baseball. The club mark pays homage to this lineage: sugar cane green for the fields, and straight lines for the industrial roots of the region.

Grounds

Estadio Tetelo Vargas San Pedro, D.R.

Known locally as “The House That Sugar Built.” It is small, humid, and loud. The outfield dimensions are standard, but the heavy sea air from the Caribbean often suppresses fly balls in the late innings.

Tradition

The city is known as “The Cradle of Shortstops,” having produced more elite infielders per capita than any other municipality on earth. The club maintains this philosophy: defense is not a skill, it is an inheritance.

Timeline

  • Late 19th Century Arrival of the “Cocolo” migrants to the sugar industry.
  • 2024 Charter Member of Global League Baseball.
  • 2026 Hosted the inaugural GLB Caribbean Series.
