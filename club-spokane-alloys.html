01 The Club

Spokane’s club.

The Spokane Alloys joined Global League Baseball in 2019 and quickly became one of the Pacific Division’s defining clubs. Their identity draws from the region’s industrial history, changing skyline and enduring relationship with the Spokane River.

The name reflects strength through combination: different elements brought together to create something durable. That idea is visible throughout the club’s home, its visual language and the supporters who fill the River District on game days.

02 Home Ground

OGWA Stadium

An open-air, 20,000-seat ballpark in Spokane’s River District, OGWA Stadium pairs a compact baseball footprint with broad concourses, river-facing views and sea-glass light throughout the structure.

03 At the Ballpark

Details that belong to Spokane.

Ballpark Feature

The Rain Wall

A public refill wall built into the main concourse, designed as both a gathering point and a practical part of the stadium.

Club Tradition

Monthly Bottle Night

Once each homestand cycle, supporters receive a limited Spokane edition reusable bottle produced for the club.

Visual Identity

Sea-Glass Light

Soft green-blue lighting runs through the stadium after sunset, giving night games a distinct River District atmosphere.
