After the Ninth

OGWA Stadium

A ballpark sitting empty after the ninth inning is, according to the people who run OGWA Stadium, an accounting failure.

The resulting property schedule includes baseball, a monthly two-piece, reusable-bottle distribution, hydraulic-car measurement, and several uses that are described in contracts only as “stadium operations.”

DONK

Sanctioned Event · OGWA Stadium

DONK operates independently of Global League Baseball. Under a venue agreement, selected circuit events are staged at GLB parks after the baseball schedule clears. Competition is divided into separate disciplines; a driver may win a category without winning the event.

Driver Entry · Spokane Regional

Pineberry campaigns an OGWA-branded 1986 Chevrolet Caprice Classic. The sea-glass paint, chrome, and water-drip graphics remain visible until the hydraulics engage.

Height runs at OGWA use a fixed measuring rig positioned beside the competition lane. The reading is recorded in inches.

Driver Entry · Spokane Regional

Vic Katz

Katz campaigns a 1985 Chevrolet Citation for Star Castle Family Fun Centers, a locally owned party-rental company supplying inflatable castles, bounce houses, folding tables, yard signs, and concession machines across the Spokane area.

The Citation carries balloons, chrome stars, confetti, and the smiling Star Castle mascot. Katz wears the matching patch on a black jacket. No alternate jacket has been observed.

SONAR SYSTEM

Monthly Resident Entertainment
Resident Artist · Spokane Alloys

SONAR SYSTEM

SONAR SYSTEM is the Spokane Alloys' monthly resident musical act: a stripped-down male-female two-piece built around guitar, drums, distortion, volume, and the kind of raw Northwest sound that feels native to the industrial edges of OGWA Stadium.

Bea Beatty fronts the duo on guitar and vocals. Pauly Czykowski plays drums. There is no large backing band and very little polish added for the sake of the venue. The attraction is the friction between two people making more noise than two people should reasonably be able to make.

Their dates appear on the recurring stadium calendar between baseball operations and facility holds. The arrangement is listed as “monthly resident entertainment.” No further description is provided.

Broadcast Team

Voices of the Alloys
Play-by-Play

Martin Vale

Martin Vale is the primary voice of Spokane Alloys baseball. His measured, observational play-by-play has become part of the atmosphere of games at OGWA Stadium, particularly during the colder and wetter stretches of the Spokane season.

Analyst

Graham Mercer

Graham Mercer works alongside Vale as Spokane's regular analyst. His conversational style and tendency to linger on the smaller details of a game provide the counterweight to Vale's play-by-play.

Around OGWA

Approved finish

The Rain Wall
