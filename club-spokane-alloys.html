After the Ninth

OGWA Stadium

A ballpark sitting empty after the ninth inning is, according to the people who run OGWA Stadium, an accounting failure.

The resulting property schedule includes baseball, a monthly two-piece, reusable-bottle distribution, hydraulic-car measurement, and several uses that are described in contracts only as “stadium operations.”

Resident Artist · Spokane Alloys

SONAR SYSTEM

SONAR SYSTEM is the Spokane Alloys' monthly resident musical act: a stripped-down male-female two-piece built around guitar, drums, distortion, volume, and the kind of raw Northwest sound that feels native to the industrial edges of OGWA Stadium.

Bea Beatty fronts the duo on guitar and vocals. Pauly Czykowski plays drums. There is no large backing band and very little polish added for the sake of the venue. The attraction is the friction between two people making more noise than two people should reasonably be able to make.

Their dates appear on the recurring stadium calendar between baseball operations and facility holds. The arrangement is listed as “monthly resident entertainment.” No further description is provided.

Game Voices

Voices of the Alloys
Play-by-Play

Martin Vale

Martin Vale is the primary voice of Spokane Alloys baseball. His measured, observational play-by-play has become part of the atmosphere of games at OGWA Stadium, particularly during the colder and wetter stretches of the Spokane season.

In-Stadium Announcer

Graham Mercer

Graham Mercer is the in-stadium voice of OGWA Stadium. He handles lineups, player introductions, scoring announcements, promotions, and the rhythm between pitches. His voice belongs to the park, not the broadcast booth.

Around OGWA
