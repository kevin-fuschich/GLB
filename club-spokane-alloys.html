The Club

Spokane’s club file reads like a shift report: innings logged, work completed, no extra motion. The Alloys don’t “announce” themselves. They show up, hold the line, and let the game get quiet.

  • Style: —
  • Vibe: Cold Industry
  • Tradition: "The Shift Change"

Ballpark

The Reduction Yards. A river-adjacent park built from utility: corrugated metal, bare concrete, and open air. It holds cold late into the season. When the wind shifts, you can smell the rail lines.

Broadcast & Announcer

Gus Vogel

Broadcast (Booth). Low register. Minimal emotion. Calls the game like a shipping report.

Cal “Switchboard” Mercer

In-Stadium Announcer. Fast cadence, clean calls, never hams it up. Sounds like a factory intercom that learned baseball.

Mascot & Stands

Ingot

A hulking, human-worn figure built from patched plating and riveted scrap. Not theatrical. Not cute. More like a walking warning sign that occasionally high-fives a kid.

The Freeze Line

Right-field regulars who “stand quiet” through entire innings. No heckling. No wave. The noise comes only when there’s a strikeout—like a stamp of approval.
