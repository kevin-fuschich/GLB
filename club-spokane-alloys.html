The Club

Spokane’s club record reads like municipal paperwork. The Alloys are not defined by slogans or eras, but by repetition—night games, cool air, long innings, and a crowd that understands patience as participation.

  • Founded: Record Pending
  • Home: The Reduction Yards

Ballpark

The Reduction Yards sit near the river, exposed to evening chill and open sky. The field is plain, functional, and active—bases worn, grass trimmed tight, lights humming into the night.

Broadcast & Announcer

Gus Vogel

Radio & Television. Vogel delivers the game plainly. No mythology, no flourish—just counts, calls, and silence when it matters.

In-Stadium Announcer: Mark Halvorsen. Known locally for short sentences and a voice that never competes with the crowd.

Mascot & Stands

Ingot

A long-running costume worn by dozens over the years. Ingot does not perform routines. He walks the aisles, acknowledges kids, and leans on railings.

The Freeze Line

Fans stand through entire innings. Noise is brief and purposeful. Applause comes late.
