Spokane Alloys

Americas Division · Spokane, Washington · Active Club Record

Ballpark

The Alloys play in a working river city stadium framed by rail lines, mills, and elevated steel. Night games are defined by hard shadows, metallic light, and the constant presence of freight movement beyond the outfield.

Broadcast & Announcer

Gus Vogel has called Alloys games for over a decade. His broadcasts favor pace and clarity over embellishment, often allowing long stretches of crowd and field sound to carry the game.

The booth overlooks the third-base line, with an unobstructed view of play and the yard beyond left field.

In the Stands

Attendance reflects the city itself: shift workers, families, long-time season holders. Crowd response tends toward collective rhythm rather than spectacle.
