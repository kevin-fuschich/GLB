The Club

The Alloys are defined by patience and compression. Born from aluminum smelters and river labor, Spokane treats baseball as an industrial process rather than a spectacle. Games are quiet, methodical, and unforgiving.

  • Style: Pitching / Low Output
  • Vibe: Cold Industry
  • Tradition: “The Shift Change”

Ballpark

A utilitarian riverside structure. Metal seating retains cold well into summer. Luxury is absent by design.

Broadcast

Gus Vogel

Voice of the Alloys. Informational, restrained, and exact. Broadcasts feel like inventory reports — deliberate and unemotional.
