The Club

The Spokane Alloys are a long-standing GLB club tied closely to the region’s industrial and riverfront history. Home games are played in low light, cold air, and a stadium built for durability rather than spectacle. The club is known less for ceremony than for routine.

Ballpark

The Reduction Yards. A concrete-and-metal ballpark near the river. Cold surfaces, minimal signage, and exposed structure define the experience.

Broadcast & Announcer

Gus Vogel — Broadcast

Vogel calls games with long pauses and restrained delivery. The broadcast favors accuracy over emotion.

Harlan Pike — Stadium Announcer

The in-park voice of the Alloys. Flat cadence. No music cues. Announcements echo through concrete and steel.

Mascot & Stands

Ingot

A silent, utilitarian presence near the bullpen. Appears without ceremony.
