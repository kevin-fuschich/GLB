The Spokane Club

Beyond the Box Score

The Spokane Alloys are built around more than the baseball club itself. OGWA Stadium has become a gathering place for a distinctly Spokane mix of baseball, industrial design, music, custom machinery, and recurring events that give the club its own rhythm.

The team page remains the official baseball record. This club page is where the other parts of Spokane live: supporters, broadcasters, recurring entertainment, stadium traditions, special events, and the pieces of culture that have grown around the Alloys.

DONK

Independent Car Show League
Spokane DONK Entry

Luthar Pineberry

DONK is a separate competitive car-show league whose events are hosted at selected GLB parks. Drivers and builders compete across judged categories involving custom builds, presentation, engineering, identity, and spectacle.

Spokane's entry is driven by Luthar Pineberry, whose 1986 Chevrolet Caprice Classic carries a candy-saturated SweeTARTS-inspired identity: bright color, oversized wheels, polished surfaces, and a deliberately excessive competition presence.

The point is not simply to own an unusual car. DONK competitors build a complete identity around the machine, then defend it against rivals across the competition calendar.

SONAR SYSTEM

Monthly Resident Entertainment
Resident Artist · Spokane Alloys

SONAR SYSTEM

SONAR SYSTEM is the Spokane Alloys' monthly resident musical act: a stripped-down male-female two-piece built around guitar, drums, distortion, volume, and the kind of raw Northwest sound that feels native to the industrial edges of OGWA Stadium.

Bea Beatty fronts the duo on guitar and vocals. Pauly Czykowski plays drums. There is no large backing band and very little polish added for the sake of the venue. The attraction is the friction between two people making more noise than two people should reasonably be able to make.

Their appearances are part of Spokane's recurring club calendar rather than one-off concert bookings, making SONAR SYSTEM part of the Alloys' identity in the same way the stadium, supporters, and matchday rituals are.

Broadcast Team

Voices of the Alloys
Play-by-Play

Martin Vale

Martin Vale is the primary voice of Spokane Alloys baseball. His measured, observational play-by-play has become part of the atmosphere of games at OGWA Stadium, particularly during the colder and wetter stretches of the Spokane season.

Analyst

Graham Mercer

Graham Mercer works alongside Vale as Spokane's regular analyst. His conversational style and tendency to linger on the smaller details of a game provide the counterweight to Vale's play-by-play.
