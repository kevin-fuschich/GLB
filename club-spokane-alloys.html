The Club

The Alloys are defined by patience and compression. Born from the region's aluminum smelters, the club treats baseball as an industrial process. They are known for a suffocating, low-scoring style of play that mirrors the damp, heavy atmosphere of the river valley. Wins are manufactured, not gifted.

  • Field Notes: River Damp
  • Home Trait: Quiet Pressure
  • Ritual: "The Shift Change"

Ballpark

The Reduction Yards. A utilitarian structure adjacent to the river, often shrouded in early morning fog. The metal seating retains the cold well into June. There are no luxury boxes, only "Foreman's Risers" along the first base line.

Broadcast & Announcer

Lead Voice. Long pauses. Clipped delivery. Doesn’t sell the moment.

Mascot & Stands

Ingot

A silent, hulking figure constructed from recycled smelter plating. Ingot does not perform; he stands sentry near the bullpen, a reminder of the raw material the city produces.

The Freeze Line

Spokane fans dress for winter regardless of the season. They are known for their "quiet innings"—entire frames played in absolute silence until a strikeout is recorded.
