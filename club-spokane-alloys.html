The Club

The Spokane Alloys maintain a deliberate, workmanlike approach to the game. Nothing flashy, nothing rushed. Games here feel earned—measured in innings, pitch counts, and long defensive stretches that reward patience.

Ballpark

The Reduction Yards. A river-adjacent park built of steel, concrete, and exposed structure. Bat cracks echo sharply. Gloves pop loud in the evening air.

Broadcast & Announcers

Gus Vogel

Play-by-play. Calm, precise, and unembellished. Vogel calls the game like a ledger—every pitch accounted for.

Ray Calder

In-stadium announcer. A steady baritone voice that carries cleanly through the steel grandstands. Lineups, counts, substitutions—nothing wasted.

Mascot & Stands

Ingot

A long-standing fixture of the park. Ingot doesn’t perform routines— he wanders, acknowledges, and looms quietly near the bullpen.

The Freeze Line

Fans here watch closely. Gloves out, scorecards open. Applause comes late—but it means something when it arrives.
