The Club

The Frost operate quietly. Games in St. Paul stretch long, shaped by cold air, hard ground, and an audience that watches without rushing the moment. Nothing is sold as identity here—only recorded. Missed swings sting more. Line drives feel heavier. When the crowd reacts, it’s earned.

Ballpark

Riverfront Ice Yard. Open sightlines, exposed concrete, and late-season chill that settles in after the fourth inning. Breath shows. Gloves stiffen. The lights feel brighter against the dark.

Broadcast & Announcers

Mascot & Stands

Rime

A padded, human-worn figure in layered blues and whites. Rime moves slowly, leaning into the cold—waving, nodding, posing for photos with gloved kids along the aisle.

The North Rail

Blankets over laps, thermoses under seats. Fans stay late, even when the temperature drops. Applause arrives in bursts—short, sharp, and sincere.
