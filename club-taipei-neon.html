The Club

The Neon play under constant motion. Rain, traffic, signage, crowds — nothing in Taipei ever fully stops. The club reflects that energy with speed, pressure, and improvisation. Games feel less like innings and more like long nights that never quite reset.

  • Style: Speed / Pressure
  • Vibe: Night City
  • Tradition: “Last Train”

Ballpark

Lantern Field. A compact stadium woven into the city grid. LED boards glow through mist and drizzle. Street noise bleeds into the park. Night games feel continuous with the city around them.

Broadcast

Chen & Lu

Voices of the Neon. Fast, conversational, bilingual at times. Calls are layered — play-by-play mixed with crowd reaction, street noise, and music drifting in from outside the park.

Mascot & Stands

Glow

A human-worn mascot built for night games — reflective trim, worn fabric, visible seams. Glow moves constantly through the aisles, rarely stopping, always in motion.

The Night Crowd

After-work fans, students, families, vendors. Phones glow. Food steams. Applause comes in waves instead of bursts.
