The Club

Toledo plays with a hard-edged patience. It’s not loud for the sake of loud— it’s loud because the game is close enough to touch. Long at-bats, tight defense, and a kind of stubborn momentum that shows up late.

Ballpark

The Glassworks. Clean lines, bright angles, exposed structure. The sound carries—bat cracks and glove pops cut through the air like they’ve got somewhere to be.

Broadcast & Announcers

Cal Mercer

Play-by-play. Quick, specific, and local to the bone. Mercer calls the inning like he’s lived inside it.

Joan Rusk

In-stadium announcer. Crisp, clean, and slightly unforgiving. Names land sharp. Counts land sharper.

Mascot & Stands

Shatter

A park fixture with a glass-helmet stare. Shatter doesn’t do routines— just appears, watches, and drifts along the rail like a rumor.

The River Rail

Fans here track the game like it’s personal. Late applause, loud opinions, and an instinct for pressure moments that makes the park feel smaller.
