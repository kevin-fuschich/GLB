Vancouver

Est. 2024 • Canada

Identity

The city is the Pacific terminus of the rail line and the gateway to the East. It exists in the space between the mountains and the ocean, defined by a perpetual state of transit. Everything here is either arriving or departing.

The name “Current” refers not only to the waters of the Burrard Inlet but to the hydroelectric power that drives the grid. The club identity mirrors the climate: persistent, enveloping, and fluid.

Grounds

Terminal Field Vancouver, B.C.

Constructed on reclaimed land at the edge of the shipping container yards. The stadium is open to the north, allowing the damp harbor air to settle onto the field at night. The sound of distant foghorns is a constant backdrop to play.

Tradition

The club rejects rigidity. In a city where rain is not weather but atmosphere, play continues through conditions that would halt games elsewhere. The team philosophy emphasizes adaptability and fluid motion over static power.

Timeline

  • 1887 Arrival of the first transcontinental train at the waterfront.
  • Late 20th Century Expansion of the deep-sea container terminals.
  • 2024 Charter Member of Global League Baseball.
