Toledo

Est. 2024 • Ohio

Identity

The city developed around silica and heat. Known as the “Glass City,” its industrial base was built on the furnaces that supplied the automotive boom. The club mirrors the physics of the manufacturing floor: material tempered by fire to withstand structural load.

Grounds

Furnace Field Toledo, Ohio

Set near the Maumee River with rail infrastructure in reach. The park is spare: exposed steel, reclaimed brick, and hard shadows that shift across the infield as the light fades. Wind off the water can flatten high contact and turn routine flies into work.

Tradition

The club follows the assembly-line ethic. Outcomes matter more than moments. The preferred style is disciplined and repeatable: take the extra base, finish the play, return the ball. Flash is tolerated; reliability is rewarded.

Timeline

  • Late 19th Century Rapid expansion of the glass manufacturing industry in the region.
  • Mid-20th Century Peak production period for automotive glass and parts.
  • 2024 Charter Member of Global League Baseball.
