Vancouver

Est. 2024 • Canada

Identity

Vancouver exists at the edge of the continent. The city was shaped less by conquest than by logistics—timber, shipping lanes, and the quiet movement of goods between ocean and rail.

The club identity follows that logic. It favors patience, range, and endurance. Nothing here is rushed; everything is moved.

Grounds

Harbor Park Vancouver, BC

The park sits low and close to the water. Air density and persistent moisture suppress carry, rewarding line drives and penalizing excess lift. Night games often dissolve into fog by the seventh inning.

Tradition

The club reflects the port itself. Roles are specialized. Errors are remembered. Success is measured not by spectacle, but by throughput.

Timeline

  • Late 19th Century Vancouver emerges as a Pacific shipping terminal.
  • 20th Century Expansion of rail and maritime trade defines the city.
  • 2024 Charter Member of Global League Baseball.
