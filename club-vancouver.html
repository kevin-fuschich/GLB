Vancouver

Est. 2024 • British Columbia

Identity

Vancouver exists at the edge of the continent, where industry, water, and weather negotiate daily. The city was built outward from its harbor, shaped by shipping lanes, timber exports, and the constant presence of rain.

The club identity reflects this restraint. There is no appetite for excess. Precision, patience, and silence define both the city and its approach to the game.

Grounds

Harbor Grounds Vancouver, Canada

Situated near the working waterfront, the park is frequently wrapped in fog. Night games are played under muted light, with the sound of container ships idling beyond the outfield wall.

Tradition

The club values control over momentum. Errors are viewed as structural failures, not emotional lapses. The prevailing belief is that if a game is played correctly, it will end quietly in your favor.

Timeline

  • 1887 Incorporation of the City of Vancouver.
  • Early 20th Century Expansion of Pacific shipping and timber trade.
  • 2024 Charter Member of Global League Baseball.
