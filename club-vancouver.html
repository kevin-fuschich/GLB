Vancouver

Est. 2024 • Canada

Identity

Vancouver exists at the edge of circulation. It is a terminal city—where land meets ocean, and where goods, people, and weather arrive before moving elsewhere.

The club’s identity is drawn from this constant exchange. Nothing here is static. Rosters change, conditions shift, and visibility is never guaranteed. The organization accepts uncertainty as a condition of play.

Grounds

Harbor Park Vancouver, Canada

The park sits low to the water. Fog rolls in without warning, flattening depth perception and dampening carry. Night games often feel enclosed, as if played inside a cloud.

Tradition

The club values restraint. Aggression is measured, not celebrated. Pitching is built around control and endurance; defense is trained for long intervals of attention without action.

Success here is quiet and often unnoticed.

Timeline

  • Late 19th Century Development of the harbor as a Pacific trade gateway.
  • 20th Century Expansion into one of North America’s primary port cities.
  • 2024 Charter Member of Global League Baseball.
