Vancouver

Est. 2024 • Canada

Identity

Vancouver exists at the edge of the continent, where mountains terminate abruptly into water. The city was built not inward, but outward—defined by shipping lanes, fog, and the constant movement of goods across the Pacific.

The club identity reflects this position. It is outward-facing, patient, and deliberate, shaped by tides rather than crowds.

Grounds

Harbor Field Vancouver, Canada

Situated near the working port, the park is frequently wrapped in low cloud cover. Moist air dampens flight paths and softens sound, producing a game that feels slower and heavier than its dimensions suggest.

Tradition

The club values restraint. Flash is considered unnecessary; efficiency is respected. The game is treated as a sequence of decisions made under imperfect visibility.

Timeline

  • Late 19th Century Development of the harbor as a Pacific trade gateway.
  • 20th Century Expansion of maritime and rail infrastructure.
  • 2024 Charter Member of Global League Baseball.
