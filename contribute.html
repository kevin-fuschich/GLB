Have an idea beyond the artifacts?

Have an idea for a new character, business, event, place or something GLB hasn’t imagined yet? Send it to the GLB Board here. You don’t need to choose an existing artifact. Bigger ideas go to the Board for review and consideration for the commissioner’s desk. Tell us what you have in mind and where it fits — or leave room for us to figure that out together. You can also use this form for a longer story, artwork or supporting material.
