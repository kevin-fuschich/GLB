# 2026 calendar

The regular season runs March 8–September 12. Postseason play runs September 15–October 6. The offseason begins October 7.

`games.json` is the authoritative regular-season index. `date` is the official GLB game date. `source` points to the immutable original box-score record; its directory and raw date may reflect the earlier archive calendar. `sourceDate` preserves that original date. Resolve box scores through `source`, and display the index date. Regular-season results and player totals have not changed.

Postseason games and their box scores use the final calendar directly, with separate season totals.
