# Compact club pages

Santiago is the reference. The shared compact-clubs enhancement applies to the other fifteen teams through their team and club entry pages, including the generic team route.

Existing collections initially show four cards or player records, with a control for the complete set. Roster and leader tables open on selection. Park features become image previews with expandable complete records. Content, identities, imagery, claim forms and stable anchors remain attached to their original elements. Without JavaScript the original complete record remains readable.

This is a presentation change. It does not assert that unfinished clubs have new card art or park content. Santiago retains its custom compact implementation.
