# GLB RECORD PAGE SPECIFICATION

This document defines the permanent rules for all GLB **Record Pages**.

Record Pages exist to document outcomes, not to promote teams, players, or the league itself.

They are archival by design.

---

## What a Record Page Is

A Record Page is an official, factual document.

It may include:
- Team name
- Date
- Division
- Record (W–L)
- Runs scored / runs allowed
- Roster listings
- Recent game results
- Official box score references

The tone is neutral, restrained, and non-promotional.

---

## What a Record Page Is Not

A Record Page is **not** a club page, profile page, or marketing surface.

Record Pages must **never** include:
- Team logos
- Team colors beyond system defaults
- Photography or illustrations
- Mascots or iconography
- Marketing copy or slogans
- Narrative hype or fan-facing language

Records do not celebrate.
They document.

---

## Visual Standards

- Typography is limited to system serif and sans-serif fonts.
- Layout prioritizes alignment, whitespace, and legibility.
- Color usage is restricted to system neutrals.
- Decorative elements are prohibited.

Consistency is mandatory.

---

## Language Standards

- Use “team” for records.
- Use “club” only as a forward reference to future materials.
- Avoid adjectives that imply quality, momentum, or emotion.
- Dates and results are stated plainly.

---

## Relationship to Club Pages

Record Pages and Club Pages are distinct.

- Record Pages document what happened.
- Club Pages express who a team is.

Club Pages may include logos, color, history, and narrative.
Record Pages may not.

---

## Permanence

These rules are not seasonal.

All Record Pages — past, present, and future — follow this specification.

If a page violates this document, the page is incorrect.
