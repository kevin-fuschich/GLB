# GLB — Season Specification

## Definition
A season is a bounded competitive period defined by:
- Official opening date
- Official closing date
- Accumulated official results

## Binding Rules
- Team Record Pages are implicitly bound to the active season.
- Season is inferred unless explicitly stated.
- No team record spans multiple seasons.

## Versioning
- Seasons are identified by year (e.g., 2026).
- Future seasons may introduce structural changes.
- Past seasons remain frozen.

## Archival Principle
Once a season closes:
- All Team Record Pages are frozen.
- No retroactive edits except scoring corrections.

## Status
Active season rules apply unless superseded.
