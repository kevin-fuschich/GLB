A new week. A fresh chance.

Pick four.

One pitcher and three position players. No draft budget. Pick from any GLB club.

Lock your team.

Entries close Monday at midnight, New York time, before the week’s games. Change your picks until then. After the deadline, your team stays fixed through Sunday.

Win a pack.

The highest total wins one FIELDSTOCK pack. Teams tied for first each win a pack. Scores reset for every tournament. Entry is free.

How players score points

Batters: total bases + runs + RBI + walks + 2 × stolen bases.

Pitchers: outs recorded + strikeouts − 2 × earned runs + 5 × wins + 3 × saves. Points can be negative.

Only the tournament’s scheduled GLB games count. A player who does not appear earns zero. Results remain provisional until all included games are recorded and the GLB Board confirms the week. A postponed included game keeps the award pending until its result is recorded.

Winners can collect their pack into their book. If packs are not yet released or inventory is exhausted, the reward stays pending until a pack is available.

Season scoring and results

Batters: total bases + runs + RBI + walks + 2 × stolen bases. Pitchers: outs recorded + strikeouts − 2 × earned runs + 5 × wins + 3 × saves.

Scores remain provisional until all included games are recorded and the GLB Board confirms the season. A postponed included game keeps the final result pending. Season-long standings are separate from weekly tournaments and their pack rewards.
