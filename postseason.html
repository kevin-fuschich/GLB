Six teams: two division winners with byes and four wild cards. Wild Card best of three, Division Series best of five, GLB Championship Series best of seven. Division winners seed 1–2 by record; wild cards seed 3–6 by record. Ties break on run differential, then club slug. After the Wild Card round, seed 1 faces the lowest remaining seed. Higher seed hosts all Wild Card games, Division Series games 1, 2 and 5, and Championship games 1, 2, 6 and 7.

Postseason scores and inning records are recorded separately from regular-season standings and player totals. Individual postseason player lines were not recorded.
