The Kiln Grounds

Home Ground

The Kiln Grounds. Pale concrete terraces hold the afternoon light. Colorful pennants cross the open bowl; pink, yellow, and turquoise move through the stands. Sound carries across the hard surfaces, from the next pitch to the horns along the rail.

The Club

Concrete · Color · The Stands

The Cementeros play in cream uniforms with hot-pink sleeves. Around them: sunlit concrete, mirrored sunglasses, white face paint, patterned umbrellas, and horns held above the rail. The club’s name stays grounded in cement; its matchday belongs to the people filling the bowl. Manager Tomás “Nivel” Aranda leads the dugout.

The Mix Line

Pink and orange horns cross above the rail. Supporters wear white face paint and hold patterned umbrellas against the afternoon sun. The Mix Line keeps its color and sound between innings.

Mascot & Stands

Field Level

El Testigo

A long face, two dark eyes, dust in the seams. El Testigo waits at the tunnel with a faded pink strip tied around one wrist. The innings change; his expression barely does.

The Mix Line

Face paint, parasols, sunglasses, and bright horns. Friends crowd the rail, and the colors carry from row to row.

DONK operates independently of Global League Baseball. Selected circuit events use GLB parks under venue agreements. At The Kiln Grounds, hydraulic height runs take place on a hard-surface competition lane, with the measuring rig beside the car.

Driver Entry · Monterrey

Octavio “Tavo” Saldaña

Saldaña brings a 1979 Plymouth Fury III in pearl cream and hot pink, finished with a thin turquoise pinstripe and polished chrome. He works the wired controller from beside the lane, watching the nose rather than the crowd.

The crew calls the car La Mezcladora. Tavo carries a folded polishing cloth in his shirt pocket; after each run, he checks the chrome before looking at the measurement.
