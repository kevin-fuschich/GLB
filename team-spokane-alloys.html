Club Identity

Spokane Club File

The Spokane Alloys represent a city shaped by industry, water, rail, and the inland Northwest. The club identity is built around durability, composition, and the idea that strong materials are created by bringing different elements together.

Spokane plays at OGWA Stadium in the River District, where sea-glass lighting, open-air concourses, and the stadium’s Rain Wall refill station have become defining parts of the club’s public identity.
