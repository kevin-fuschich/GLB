Club Identity

Spokane Club File

Founded as one of the sixteen charter clubs of Global League Baseball, the Spokane Alloys represent Spokane, Washington, and compete in the Pacific Division. The club plays at OGWA Stadium in the River District and draws its identity from the Inland Northwest's history of industry, rail, manufacturing, and craftsmanship.
